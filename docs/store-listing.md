## Title

Two Kings Chess

## Short description

Random armies, two kings, and comeback powers.

## Description

Two kings protect each side. Random starting armies make every match different, and falling behind lets you draft powers that change what your pieces can do. Play the computer at three difficulty levels or share one device for pass-and-play.

Try daily challenges, a ranked ladder and puzzles. Use hints, Undo and game review freely. Choose a board, piece style and comeback aura; cosmetics can be earned through play. This build also offers free cosmetic previews saved on your device. It takes no payment.
